How lessons work

Three calm steps

  1. Say what you want to learn

    A short note is enough. “My phone confuses me” is a fine place to start.

  2. Meet in person or by video

    Rikki sits beside you, or joins by video. You hold your own device and do the tapping. Rikki guides.

  3. Keep the steps

    After a lesson, the steps are written down for you in large type. The lesson is still there next week.

How Rikki teaches

Plain words

No jargon. Every new word gets explained once, in everyday language, and written down.

Your pace

Ask the same question as many times as you like. Repeating a step is how learning works.

Your privacy

You type your own passwords. Rikki looks away. Nothing on your device is opened without your permission.
